# Native working capital

Razor's optional working-capital workflow connects a company with explicitly eligible lenders. It uses a separate shared NEAR credit escrow with canonical native NEAR USDC. Supported-asset company funding and employee/supplier payouts keep their ordinary choices; this loan mode does not expand the assets accepted by its escrow.

An existing operator publishes actual company/lender eligibility, responsibilities, network, contract, token and treasury identities through `scripts/financing-scope.ts`. The operator must already exist in Razor and be allowlisted by `RAZOR_FINANCING_OPERATOR_USER_IDS`. Published scopes live in the database and are versioned. An empty scope list means lending is unavailable. Publishing a scope does not provide a lender, guarantee capital or establish production service coverage.

The borrower fixes principal, maximum total fixed cost, collateral, tenor, funding deadline, grace period, purpose, repayment source and the supported default/dispute rules. Actual eligible lenders submit immutable offers from their linked NEAR accounts. The owner accepts an exact offer and deployment-bound agreement. Normal payroll/payment authority remains separate from this acceptance.

The borrower explicitly funds loan registration storage and any bond; the lender separately transfers the principal. Only canonical token callbacks can fund these buckets. Partial deposits, returned excess, cancellation and participant refunds retain separate accounting. Advance requires the complete agreed principal and bond. The escrow sends the exact principal to the fixed company treasury using `loan:<loanId>` as its deposit reference. The application recognizes it only from final matching token and immutable treasury-credit evidence, then synchronizes the actual company balance. It never adds a locally declared loan amount to spendable cash.

Repayment is an explicit native-USDC transfer from the agreed borrower wallet. It allocates principal, then fixed interest, then the disclosed lender fee. Lender credits, borrower refunds, collateral applied and remaining shortfall stay distinct. Default can apply only this loan's bond under its agreed due/grace rule. Dispute requires the supported bilateral resumption; it cannot reprice the loan or authorize treasury-wide access. Uncertain transfers remain held until their exact callback or supported positive advance evidence establishes an outcome. There is no timeout-based withdrawal reset.

Optional receivable backing captures an accepted original business claim and prevents competing Razor assignments, supplier credits, ownership changes and clearing discharge while assigned. Ordinary payment to the confirmed original supplier remains possible. Collection is not intercepted: the borrower collects through the normal invoice flow, converts other assets separately if necessary, and explicitly repays in native NEAR USDC. Razor does not establish external lien priority, detect every outside pledge, guarantee repayment or sweep another company's funds.

Company navigation exposes Working capital; the personal payments workspace exposes lender offers and positions. Accounting CSV includes separate loan principal, interest, fee, repayment and collateral columns. A loan advance is financing cash with a liability, not earned project revenue or a client deposit.

Build the optional artifact with `contracts/build.sh --with-credit`; include `--credit ACCOUNT` in the unsigned deployment planner. Ordinary payments require no credit deployment. Loan availability additionally needs reviewed deployment identity, token storage, published operating scope, actual lenders and real funded receipts. Source and TypeScript compilation do not establish deployed or live lending behavior. No loan was funded or advanced during this development pass.
