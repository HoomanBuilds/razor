## Integration findings from this review

- Payment-management services existed without their organization route being mounted. The mounted review routes and payroll interface now passed local HTTP and browser checks.
- A disputed invoice's encrypted reason was also copied into ordinary activity details. Activity now records status only; the private evidence checks passed. The full cross-role privacy matrix remains open.
- Resuming a saved public funding quote skipped frozen treasury-target revalidation. Resumption now revalidates authority for unsent legs and explicitly disables deposits for existing transfers and closed requests. Focused owner-removal, revocation and expiry checks passed; live funding and browser interruption recovery remain open.
- Batch approvals lacked an explicit funding summary. Review data and the interface now show available operating cash, existing reservations, uncertain transfers and shortfall, including the approved costs. Changed cash invalidates a stale review; uncertain transfers do not request duplicate funding.
- Recurring dates previously used fixed 24-hour shifts. They now preserve the company-zone wall time across daylight changes, reject nonexistent local times and hold templates when their zone or source terms change.

These findings were corrected and locally checked. No deep security audit or real-money settlement was performed. A dependency review still reports 40 advisories in the wallet dependency graph after the available nonbreaking fix attempt; forced SDK downgrades were not applied. Production dependency remediation remains open.
