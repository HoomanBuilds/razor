## Scoped accounting and company-operated private processing

Optional QuickBooks Online and Xero connectors now have credential-gated confidential OAuth, encrypted rotating grants, verified legal-entity selection, provider catalog mappings and approved posting plans. Their callbacks are `APP_URL/api/accounting-oauth/quickbooks/callback` and `APP_URL/api/accounting-oauth/xero/callback`. HTTPS consent uses a separate HttpOnly `SameSite=Lax`, `Secure`, host-only browser cookie, bound by hash to one-use state and the exact initiating Razor session. Copied links or revoked sessions cannot exchange a code, and current company permissions are checked again before retaining tokens. The ordinary Strict session cookie remains unchanged. Missing credentials never produce a connected state. Follow the [operator setup](../operator-setup.md#connect-quickbooks-online-or-xero) for credentials, registered redirects and provider scopes.

Supported provider writes are accepted business bills and verified full-principal installment accounting payments against their reconciled original bill. Mappings use actual contacts, expense/clearing accounts and verified zero-tax codes. USD, EUR, GBP, CAD, AUD, NZD, INR and SGD functional-currency books require an explicitly approved rational FX rate, source and date; USDC parity is never assumed and fractional functional cents are held. Original invoice maturity is derived using the company timezone, preserved independently of posting date and checked during reconciliation. Unscheduled invoices require an explicit reviewed provider due date without mutating the source bill.

Preparing a plan performs no financial write. Exact plan approval authorizes the bounded accounting worker, which rechecks source, mapping, selected entity and current authority. Durable leases, immutable request IDs and a pre-write journal keep uncertain writes held for read-only reconciliation. Missing provider results never authorize blind resubmission. External edits enter conflict review instead of changing local financial truth; provider statuses never establish Razor cash or token delivery. Disconnect stops future local work, removes saved tokens and separately reports whether provider revocation was confirmed. Automatic bank matching, payroll-row exports, fee/FX or credit-note journals, noncash-setoff exports and NetSuite sync remain open scope boundaries.
