### 01. Overview

> Razor brings treasury, payroll, invoices, and cross-chain payments into one workspace for global companies. Run your company's money beyond borders, with company controls built on NEAR.

### 02. Funding

> Northstar holds money across assets and chains. Choose a supported funding asset; NEAR Intents handles the conversion and routing. Razor records company obligations in USDC, while its NEAR treasury keeps each company's balances and spending rules separate. Route availability and execution costs are reviewed before payment.

### 03. Recipient choice

> Maya wants USDC on Base. Leo chooses a different supported payout asset. Each person confirms their destination and a native-USDC backup on a supported network. If the preferred route is unavailable, that consented backup provides another option. An uncertain transfer must be reconciled before trying again.

### 04. Payroll

> Here is Northstar's payroll. Review each person's entitlement, the destinations, and the maximum execution cost. Approve the run, then follow each payment through its own status. The company balance updates, completed obligations close, and individual payment records stay traceable.

### 05. Receiver proof

> Now switch to Maya. The same payment appears in her personal workspace with her chosen destination. Leo sees his payment too, alongside work for another company. Recipients manage their own payout preferences and history without gaining access to any company's treasury.

### 06. Intercompany clearing

> Razor also reduces the money businesses need to move between each other. These three companies owe twenty-seven hundred USDC in invoices. With every company consenting, this cash-backed clearing example needs six hundred USDC of net backing. That is less capital to move, not a claim that twenty-seven hundred tokens were transferred.

### 07. Controls and optional privacy

> Safety stays inside the workflow. An unknown payment is held for reconciliation, so a retry cannot silently become a second payout. Sensitive invoice processing can use optional NEAR AI Cloud. Your team still reviews the invoice and approves payment; private AI is not required to use Razor.

### 08. Comparison

> Razor takes zero percentage markup on payment principal. This comparison keeps the differences visible: PayPal's listed merchant checkout fees, Request's zero stablecoin processing, and Rise's contractor services. These products have different scopes; network and conversion costs still matter.

### 09. Business model

> We earn through optional workspace subscriptions: Free, Pro at twenty-nine USDC, and Business at ninety-nine USDC per month. The current pilot includes complimentary Pro access. Companies pay for workflow capabilities, while recipients keep access to their payment history and preferences.

### 10. Close

> NEAR treasury controls. NEAR Intents routing. Optional private AI. Razor brings them into one company payment workflow. Explore Razor at razorfi dot xyz.

## Dataset and continuity

- Northstar Studio is the principal payer. Harbor Labs and Meridian Creative are fictional counterparties.
- Maya Chen and Leo Martins have distinct local identities, linked receiving profiles and different payout preferences. Another company can pay the same receiver through a separate profile.
- Keep USDC entitlement, payout-token amount, source spend and fees distinct. A non-USDC receiving amount must not be labelled as USDC.
- A funded payroll remains affordable after fees and reservations. The same payment IDs and amounts appear on payer and receiver screens. Repeating a settlement control does not debit a second time.
- For the clearing example, Northstar owes Harbor 1,200, Harbor owes Meridian 900, and Meridian owes Northstar 600 USDC. Net positions require Northstar to back 600, with 300 due to each other company. Debt-only cancellation of 1,800 and residual invoice face of 900 are separate quantities; they are not the cash-backed funding requirement.
- Use coherent recent history and a prepared current payroll. Completed historical records are fictional seed data, not observed customer usage.
- Maintain reset points per cut. The recording sandbox must never read production credentials, write the production database, sign transactions, contact paid inference, send emails or publish accounting entries.
